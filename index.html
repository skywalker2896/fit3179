How to read this

Each player is placed in the column for the round their seed predicts they will reach (seeds 1–4: SF, 5–8: QF, 9–16: R16, 17–32: R32, unseeded: R1/R2) and the row for the round they actually exited.

Players in a row above their column's round outperformed their seed; players below it underperformed.
